# Persona: Integrator

## Profile

A person responsible for connecting the project to another system, service, application, or operational environment. They may be a developer, administrator, or technical specialist.

## Goals

- Establish a compatible and reliable connection between systems.
- Understand interfaces, dependencies, and configuration boundaries.
- Diagnose integration failures and manage compatibility changes.

## Knowledge

### Assumed knowledge

Basic understanding of software interfaces, dependencies, configuration, and system interactions.

### Required knowledge

- Supported integration mechanisms, compatibility requirements, data exchange, and operational constraints.
- How to configure, verify, and troubleshoot the integration.

### Knowledge not to assume

- A particular protocol, language, deployment platform, or access to either system's internals.
- Upstream contribution or maintenance authority.

## Documentation needs

Integration guides, compatibility references, configuration references, interface contracts, migration and troubleshooting guides.

## Content guidelines

### Language

Use precise system-orinted language; define project-specific terms.

### Structure

Separate requirements and steps by system; include verification and failure handling.

### Context

State versions, environments, responsibilities, and boundaries between systems.

## Scope

### Include

Supported interfaces, interoperability, configuration, dependencies, verification, and lifecycle considerations.

### Exclude

Unrelated implementation internals or assumptions about a particular hosting or deployment model.
