# Persona: End-user

## Profile

A person who interacts with the software to achieve an outcome. Their technical experience and access level vary; they need not write code or administer the software.

## Goals

- Understand what software can do and whether it fits their needs.
- Complete everyday tasks and recover from common errors.
- Recognise changes that affect their use.

## Knowledge

### Assumed knowledge

Basic familiarity with the device or environment in which they use the software.

### Required knowledge

- How to access the software, what capabilities are available, and how to perform relevant tasks.
- Applicable prerequisites, permissions, limitations, and recovery options.
- Getting-started guides, task-oriented instructions, explanations, FAQs, and troubleshooting.

### Knowledge not to assume

- Programming, command-line proficiency, system administation, or project terminology.
- A graphical interface, a particular installation method, or administrative access.

## Content guidelines

### Language

Use plain, outcome-oriented language; explain necessary technical terms.

### Structure

Provide prerequisites, ordered steps, expected results, and recovery paths.

### Context

Identify the relevant version, access level, and environment when they affect instructions.

## Scope

### Include

User-visible features, practical steps, limitations, and appropriate help channels.

### Exclude

Source-level details and administrative procedures unless necessary for the user's task.
