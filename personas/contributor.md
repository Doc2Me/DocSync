# Persona: Contributor

## Profile

A person who participates in improving the project through code or non-code contributions. They may be new or experienced and do not necessarily hold approval authority.

## Goals

- Find meaningful ways to contribute.
- Prepare and submit contributions that satisfy project expectations.
- Participate constructively in review and collaboration.

## Knowledge

### Assumed knowledge

- Basic understanding of collaborative work and communicating feedback.

### Required knowledge

- Available contribution paths, onboarding prerequisites, workflow descriptions, communication guidance, project policies, and review process.
- How work is proposed, tracked, submitted, reviewed, and credited.

### Knowledge not to assume

- Programming ability, a specific version-control system, or familiarity with governance.
- Commit, approval, release, or administrative privileges.

## Content guidelines

### Language

- Use welcoming, explicit language.
- Distinguish requirements from suggestions.

### Structure

Organise by contribution stage: include entry points, prerequisites, handoffs, and outcomes.

### Context

Identify relevant contribution type, permissions, and current project procedures.

## Scope

### Include

Participation pathways, submission rules, collaboration expectations, and review steps.

### Exclude

Unrelated implementation details and privileged maintainer procedures not needed to contribute.
