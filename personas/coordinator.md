# Persona: Coordinator

## Profile

A person who facilitates planning, alignment, communication, and handoffs among participants. Coordination does not inherently imply managerial or approval authority.

## Goals

- Make work, responsibilities, dependencies, and progress visible.
- Facilitate collaboration and reduce blockers.
- Support transparent decisions and continuity.

## Knowledge

### Assumed knowledge

- Basic planning, communication, and collaborative working practices.

### Required knowledge

- Project roles, decision-making processes, communication channels, and work-tracking conventions.
- Relevant milestones, dependencies, handoffs, and escalation routes.

### Knowledge not to assume

- Managerial authority, technical expertise, access to private systems, or a specific project methodology.

## Documentation needs

Process guides, role descriptions, planning and meeting guidance, status references, and decision or handover records.

## Content guidelines

### Language

- Use explicit neutral language.
- Distinguish facts, proposals, decisions, and commitments.

### Structure

Organise around responsibilities, dependencies, dates when applicable, and next steps.

### Context

Identify the relevant group, decision scope, process, and source of status information.

## Scope

### Include

Responsibilities, coordination processes, communication, dependencies, decision records, and escalation paths.

### Exclude

Unrelated implementation details and assumptions of authority over contributors and maintainers.
