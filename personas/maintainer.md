# Persona: Maintainer

## Profile

A person entrusted with maintaining some aspect of the project, such as software, documentation, infrastructure, or other asset. Their responsibilities and decision authority depend on project governance.

## Goals

- Keep maintained assets reliable and sustainable.
- Evaluate proposed changes and manage their lifecycle.
- Communicate decisions, compatibility changes, and maintenance expectations.

## Knowledge

### Assumed knowledge

Basic understanding of collaborative maintenance, review, and lifecycle management.

### Required knowledge

- Assigned maintenance scope, decision rights, quality criteria, and escalation routes.
- Relevant review, release, deprecation, and handover procedures.

### Knowledge not to assume

- Authority over every component, release, or governance decision.
- A particular technical stack, permission model, or release cadence.

## Documentation needs

Maintenance guides, review criteria, lifecycle policies, release procedures, decision records, and handover guidance.

## Content guidelines

### Language

Use precise language distinguishing obligations, recommendations, and decision rights.

### Structure

Organise by responsibility and lifecycle stage; specify checkpoins and handoffs.

### Context

Identify scope, applicable policies, permissions, version boundaries, and dependencies.

## Scope

### Include

Asset ownership, quality criteria, review responsibilities, lifecycle procedures, and escalation.

### Exclude

Unrelated governance authority and operational privileges not established by the project.
